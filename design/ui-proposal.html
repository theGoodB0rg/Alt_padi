AltPadi · UI proposal

Calm, premium, and clear at a glance

The panel on the right is clickable. Press Check prices to watch the loading flow, then try the filters, open a card's “Why this match”, and switch scenarios below.

Scenario
Theme

  • No more spinning cursor

    The spinning circle came from the button switching to cursor: wait while searching. That tells Windows “the app is busy” and feels like a freeze. Now the cursor stays normal and the panel itself shows progress: three named steps, a thin moving line, and placeholder cards in the shape of the results. You can also stop a search.

    cursor: wait + “Searching...”Steps + skeletons + Stop

  • The answer first

    One big headline answers the only question that matters: can I pay less? The saving is the largest text on screen, with old price → new price right under it and a single “Open cheapest” button.

  • Plain English everywhere

    Labels say what they do. Technical scores are hidden until you ask for them.

    Minimum confidence: LowMatch: Any / Good / Best
    74/100 score · high▮▮▮ Best match
    Find cheaper alternativesCheck prices

  • Less to read on each card

    Each card shows four things: name, price, how much you save, and the seller's trust score. The reasons (“same brand”, “similar specs”) fold under “Why this match”.

  • Filters as one-tap chips

    The checkbox-and-dropdown box becomes one row of chips under the answer. Results update right away, with a “Clear filters” way out when nothing is left.

  • A premium look without the noise

    Warm off-white surfaces, one deep green accent, Inter type with even-width numbers, soft layered shadows, and dark mode that follows Windows. Motion is short and purposeful (cards rise in, the saving counts up, panels open smoothly), and it switches off for anyone who turns on “reduce motion”.

  • Friendly empty and error states

    “Not a product page” and “Couldn't reach Jumia” each get an icon, one sentence, and one clear next step (Retry), instead of a red text bar.
